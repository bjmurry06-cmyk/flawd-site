How it works

Honesty, in three steps.

No curated highlight reels. Flawd starts where other apps end — with the real you.

01

Own your flaws

Pick the flaws that feel uncomfortably true, and the ones you could love in a partner. Overthinker? Bad texter? Falls in love too fast? Claim it.

02

Match on truth

Our algorithm scores compatibility on real overlap — shared flaws, opposing ones, and the growth edges between you. Your flaws stay hidden until you match.

03

Start real conversations

Match, then talk. "So, what are your flaws?" is the icebreaker. No small talk, no pretending — just two people who already dropped the act.
